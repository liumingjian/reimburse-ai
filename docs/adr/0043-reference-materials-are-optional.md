# Reference materials are optional when expense details are complete

**Status: accepted (2026-11-23).** Amends the lodging classification and missing-document rules in [ADR-0006](0006-category-derived-project-chosen.md) and [ADR-0017](0017-rule-parsers-by-kind-and-layout.md), and the payment screenshot absence rule in [ADR-0032](0032-payment-screenshots-are-parsed-material.md).

A 主要报销凭证 establishes the invoice identity and amounts. 参考材料 help supply and verify the expense details. Employees can keep an invoice without its companion material by supplying any missing required details. Manual entry records details on the expense and preserves the invoice facts and the source of each value. This lets employees continue with a valid invoice while retaining the checks required for 整单明细确认 and ERP.

For all supported invoice kinds, a missing companion material offers upload, manual entry, confirmation of absence, and exclusion of the invoice. Exclusion is reversible until the first ERP action attempt. Later uploads are validated and matched using the original material rules. If valid evidence conflicts with employee-entered details, the employee resolves the conflict before reconfirming. Lodging and ride-hailing still need their actual dates and journey details, respectively. A ride-hailing invoice covering multiple trips keeps per-trip amounts that reconcile to the cent. Dining can use the invoice issue date, and the absence of a lodging payment screenshot needs no separate acknowledgement. The [product design](../design/standalone-app.md#缺少参考材料) specifies the fields and workflow.
